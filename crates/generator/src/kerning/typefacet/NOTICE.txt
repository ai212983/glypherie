Modified for Glypherie: Rust port of TypeFacet's original line, quadratic and
cubic profile construction, contour orientation, approximate segment inflation,
and intrusion predicates on explicit closed contours in font units. The original
one-decimal geometry-rounding override and fixed 16-subdivision curve evaluation
are retained. UFO I/O, sidebearing changes, category overrides and logging are
omitted. Invalid or unsupported geometry and excessive memory/work fail explicitly.
Acquiring geometry, searching placement origins, feasibility checks and rendering
remain caller policy. No automatic per-font calibration or density shift occurs.
